At {{ platform_settings.platform_name or 'PickleballHub' }}, accessible from our municipal court directories, player privacy is a primary priority. This Privacy Policy documents the types of data that we collect, record, and process when you manage an account or book courts on our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide automated court booking, coordinate tournament matchmaking brackets, and find facilities close to you, we collect data in several categories:

  • Personal Identification Data: When creating an account, we collect credentials including your full name, email address, contact phone number, and account passwords.
  • Facility & Location Info: When searching for available courts, our web application reads location parameters to calculate the nearest facilities in Laguna. This helps display courts close to your current location.
  • Transaction Logs: For confirmed reservations, the system logs checkout IDs, payment status (deposit versus full payment), and transaction timestamps. We do not store raw GCash PINs or credit card credentials.
  • Tournament Profile Details: If you compete in tournaments, we display your flight registration name, ELO standings, and DUPR verification ratings publicly on live tournament bracket boards.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data to run the platform efficiently, secure reservations, and maintain safety standards:

  • To Confirm Bookings: Reconciling reservation records to guarantee that paid court slots are secured and preventing double-booking overlaps.
  • To Calculate Proximity: Processing search terms to find the nearest courts to your region, ensuring that players match with courts within reasonable travel boundaries.
  • To Enforce Warnings: Maintaining historical warning counts (1st verbal warning, 2nd written warning) and blocklists for no-shows to ensure fair court distribution.
  • Customer Support: Verifying receipt logs and GCash confirmations to assist in cancellations, rescheduling, or refund settlements.

Note: We will never sell, lease, or rent your player profile directories or location history logs to third-party advertiser groups.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We implement a variety of security measures to keep player profiles and transactions safe:

  • Affiliated Venues: When booking a slot, the facility administrators at the selected court receive your reservation name and phone number to verify gate check-in and contact you for schedule changes.
  • Data Encryption: All password databases are hashed using industry-standard cryptography, and payment checkout redirects are secured through end-to-end SSL layers.
  • Legal Enforcement: We may share necessary details with local municipal authorities if required by law or to investigate severe vandalism to facility assets.

4. Cookies & Session Tracking

Like most modern interactive websites, our platform uses cookie identifiers to elevate the user experience:

  • Session Management: Remembering your logged-in credentials so you can navigate between courts, tournaments, and your dashboard without entering details repeatedly.
  • Preferences: Retaining your selected light or dark mode theme toggle settings across different subpages.
  • Analytics: Tracking anonymous usage patterns to optimize page load speeds, map search responsiveness, and layout layouts.

5. Your Privacy Rights

As a player on our platform, you hold control over your personal data files:

  • Data Correction: You can edit your profile name, contact details, and account password directly inside the user dashboard settings.
  • Profile Deletion: If you wish to permanently terminate your account and wipe all matching booking ledgers, you can submit a deletion ticket to our support deck.
  • Opt-Out Options: You can disable GPS location access in your browser settings, though this will limit the map's ability to display the nearest courts automatically.

6. Contacting Support

If you have additional questions regarding our privacy rules, data collection policies, or would like to report a security hazard, please contact our administrator team through the support desk in your profile dashboard.
