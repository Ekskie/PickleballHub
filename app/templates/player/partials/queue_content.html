Court Queue Etiquette

  • Arrive 10 Minutes Early

    Be at the venue ahead of your scheduled match start so court transitions flow seamlessly.

  • Keep Audio Alerts Active

    Keep this tab open; an audible chime sounds whenever you are marked Up Next or Playing.

  • Paddle Ready for "Up Next"

    When your status turns amber ("Up Next"), head to your assigned court sidelines.

Fair Play & Turnover Rules

  • Automated Game Turnover

    Standard court sessions run for the duration of the reservation with a 15-minute grace period.

  • Staff Assistance

    Need to swap courts or report an issue? Check in with the front desk facility staff.

  • Manage Your Booking

    View your upcoming schedule or cancel reservations directly from My Bookings.
